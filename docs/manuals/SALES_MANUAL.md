# LocalWheels Platform — Sales Executive Manual
Version 1.0 | Phase 19.7 | Production Release

## 1. Your Role
Sales executives manage leads, opportunities, quotations, and customer contracts in the AI-powered Sales CRM.

## 2. Lead Management

### Creating a Lead
1. **Sales CRM → Sales → Leads** — New Lead
2. Enter: Company Name, Contact, Email, Phone, Source, Estimated Value
3. AI Lead Score is assigned automatically (0–100)
4. High-score leads are prioritised in your pipeline view

### AI Lead Qualification
- The AI analyses: company size, freight volume, location match, historical data
- Score 80+ = Hot Lead — call same day
- Score 50–79 = Warm Lead — follow up within 2 days
- Score <50 = Cold Lead — nurture with email

## 3. Opportunity Pipeline
1. Convert qualified lead: **Lead → Convert to Opportunity**
2. Set: Stage, Expected Close Date, Value, Assigned Rep
3. Track pipeline: **Sales CRM → Sales → Pipeline**
4. AI Copilot suggests next best action for each opportunity

## 4. Quotation
1. **Quotes → New Quote**
2. Select customer, add freight line items, apply discounts
3. System calculates GST automatically
4. Send quote: Email / WhatsApp / Download PDF
5. Track quote status: Sent / Viewed / Accepted / Rejected

### Quote Approval
- Quotes above ₹5 lakhs require manager approval (configurable)
- **Quotes → Pending Approval** — Manager approves/rejects
- Customer receives auto-notification on approval

## 5. Sales Analytics
- **Sales CRM → Sales → Analytics** — Win rate, pipeline value, revenue by rep
- **Sales CRM → Sales → Copilot** — Ask "What's my forecast for Q3?"

## 6. Customer Contracts
- **Master → Contract → Customer** — Contract terms, rates, validity
- System auto-applies contracted rates when booking shipments for contracted customers

## 7. Weekly Sales Checklist
- [ ] Follow up on all Hot Leads
- [ ] Update opportunity stages in pipeline
- [ ] Send pending quotes
- [ ] Review won/lost deals for patterns
- [ ] Update forecast with Sales Copilot
