# LocalWheels Platform — Finance Manager Manual
Version 1.0 | Phase 19.7 | Production Release

## 1. Your Role
Finance managers handle invoicing, payments, receivables, payables, reconciliation, GST, and financial reporting.

## 2. Invoice Management

### Create Invoice
1. **Finance → Invoice Builder** — New invoice
2. Add: Customer, Line Items (freight, detention, fuel surcharge), Tax (GST 18%)
3. Preview and Send to customer

### Invoice Tracking
- **Finance → Invoice Center** — List all invoices with status
- Filter by: Paid / Unpaid / Overdue
- Send payment reminders from the invoice detail page

## 3. Accounts Receivable
- **Finance → Accounts Receivable** — Track what customers owe
- Create collection receipts when payments arrive
- Apply credit notes for disputes

## 4. Accounts Payable
- **Finance → Accounts Payable** — Track vendor payments
- Process vendor bills (transporter hire payments)
- Track TDS deductions

## 5. Bank Reconciliation
1. **Finance → Bank Reconciliation** — Upload bank statement
2. System auto-matches transactions
3. Manually match unreconciled items
4. Lock reconciled period

## 6. GST & Tax
- **Finance → GST Dashboard** — GSTR-1, GSTR-2, GSTR-3B
- **Finance → Tax Management** — TDS, TCS entries
- Export GST data for filing

## 7. Financial Reports
| Report | Location |
|--------|----------|
| Profit & Loss | Finance → Profit & Loss |
| Balance Sheet | Finance → Balance Sheet |
| Trial Balance | Finance → Trial Balance |
| Cash Flow | Finance → Cash Flow |
| General Ledger | Finance → General Ledger |
| Journal | Finance → Journal Entries |

## 8. Budget & Forecast
- **Finance → Budget Planner** — Set monthly/annual budgets per cost center
- **Finance → Financial Forecast** — AI-powered 6-month revenue forecast
- **Finance → AI Finance Copilot** — Ask any financial question in plain English

### Finance Copilot Examples
- "What is our cash position this week?"
- "Which customers have overdue invoices above ₹1 lakh?"
- "Show me branch-wise profitability for Q1"
- "What is our GST liability for June?"

## 9. Month-End Checklist
- [ ] All invoices for the month raised and sent
- [ ] Bank reconciliation complete
- [ ] All vendor bills accounted
- [ ] TDS entries made
- [ ] GST data exported
- [ ] P&L reviewed with management
- [ ] Journals balanced (Trial Balance = zero difference)
