# Quick Start Guide — Customer (Shipper/Consignee)
**LocalWheels Customer Portal v1.0**

---

## Access the Customer Portal
- URL: https://app.localwheels.com/customer-portal
- Username: As provided by LocalWheels
- Password: As provided by LocalWheels

---

## Track Your Shipment

### Option 1: With Login
1. Log in → **My Shipments**
2. See all your shipments with live status
3. Click any shipment for full timeline

### Option 2: Without Login (Public Tracking)
1. Go to https://app.localwheels.com/tracking
2. Enter LR Number or AWB Number
3. See current status and expected delivery

---

## Shipment Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Booked | LR created, awaiting pickup |
| In Transit | Goods are on the way |
| At Branch | Goods at intermediate hub |
| Out for Delivery | On vehicle, being delivered today |
| Delivered | Successfully delivered |
| Delivery Failed | Issue at delivery — contact us |

---

## Request Support
1. **My Shipments** → Click shipment → **Raise Issue**
2. Select issue type: Damaged / Delayed / Wrong Delivery / Other
3. Add description and photos
4. You'll receive a ticket number and be updated via WhatsApp

---

## Download Documents
From any shipment detail page:
- **LR Copy** → Download PDF
- **Invoice** → Download PDF
- **POD** → Download delivery proof
