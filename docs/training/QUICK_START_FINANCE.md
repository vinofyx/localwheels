# Quick Start Guide — Finance Manager
**LocalWheels v1.0** | 5-minute setup

---

## Step 1: Log In
- URL: https://app.localwheels.com
- Username: `finance_mgr` (or assigned username)
- Password: As provided by Admin

---

## Step 2: Create an Invoice
1. **Finance → Invoice Builder** → New Invoice
2. Search customer by name or phone
3. Select LR numbers to bill (system auto-fills freight charges)
4. Review: Subtotal, GST (5%/12%/18%), Total
5. Click **Generate Invoice** → Invoice number assigned
6. **Send** → Email/WhatsApp to customer, or **Download PDF**

---

## Step 3: Record Payment Received
1. **Finance → Accounts Receivable** → Find invoice
2. Click **Record Payment**
3. Enter: Amount, Payment Date, Mode (NEFT/RTGS/Cheque/Cash), Reference
4. Invoice status → Paid ✅

---

## Step 4: View Financial Reports
- **Finance → Profit & Loss** → Monthly P&L
- **Finance → Trial Balance** → Balance check
- **Finance → Cash Flow** → Cash position

---

## Step 5: GST Filing Export
1. **Finance → GST Dashboard**
2. Select Period (e.g., June 2026)
3. Click **Export GSTR-1** → Download for GST portal upload
4. Verify and file on GST portal

---

## Month-End Checklist
- [ ] All invoices raised for the month
- [ ] Payments received recorded
- [ ] Bank reconciliation completed
- [ ] Vendor bills entered in Accounts Payable
- [ ] TDS entries made
- [ ] GST data exported
- [ ] P&L reviewed
