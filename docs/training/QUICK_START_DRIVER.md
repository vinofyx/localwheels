# Quick Start Guide — Driver (Mobile App)
**LocalWheels Driver App v1.0** | 5-minute setup

---

## Download the App
- **Android:** Google Play Store → "LocalWheels Driver"
- **iOS:** App Store → "LocalWheels Driver"
- Or use the web version: https://app.localwheels.com (mobile browser)

---

## Step 1: Log In
- Username: `driver1` (or assigned username)
- Password: As provided by your dispatcher
- ⚠️ Change password on first login

---

## Step 2: Start Your Trip
1. **My Trips** → Today's assigned trip appears here
2. Tap **Start Trip** → App begins GPS tracking
3. Your live location is now visible to dispatcher

---

## Step 3: Mark Deliveries
At each delivery point:
1. Tap the delivery → **Mark Delivered**
2. Take a photo of the goods (optional but recommended)
3. Get receiver's signature on screen
4. Tap **Confirm Delivery** → POD uploaded automatically

---

## Step 4: Report Issues
If goods are damaged or refused:
1. Tap delivery → **Report Issue**
2. Select: Damaged / Shortage / Customer Absent / Refused
3. Take photo as evidence
4. Dispatcher is notified immediately

---

## Step 5: End Trip
1. Return to base
2. **My Trips** → Tap **End Trip**
3. All deliveries must be marked before ending

---

## Offline Mode
- App works offline (no internet needed during delivery)
- Data syncs automatically when internet is restored
- GPS tracking pauses offline and resumes when connected

---

## SOS (Emergency)
- Press and hold the red **SOS** button in the app
- Your location and alert is sent to dispatcher immediately
- Use only in genuine emergency

---

## Need Help?
- In-app: Menu → Help
- Call dispatcher: Listed in My Trips → Contact
